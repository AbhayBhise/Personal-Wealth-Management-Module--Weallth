# Key features for Lifestyle Planning module in wealth platforms

The Lifestyle Planning module is a critical component that transforms wealth management from pure investment management into holistic life guidance. Here are the key features based on industry best practices and leading platform implementations.

## Core Goal-Based Planning Features

**Goals Identification \& Prioritization**: The system should thoroughly evaluate an individual's financial landscape, helping to identify and prioritize specific financial goals . This includes life milestones such as buying a home, funding education, wedding planning, and retirement.[^1]

**Personalized Plan Generation**: Goals-based planning translates clients' goals into a personal and actionable plan based on a holistic overview of their financial situation. Cookie-cutter solutions are out—today's clients want advice as unique as their Netflix recommendations.[^2][^1]

### Essential Goal Categories

| Life Stage | Goal Types | Planning Features |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Early Career | Emergency fund, home purchase, wedding | Aggressive savings strategies, debt payoff planning |
| Mid-Career | Education funding, home upgrade, sabbatical | College cost projectors, second home analysis |
| Pre-Retirement | Retirement readiness, healthcare, downsizing | Income replacement, Medicare planning |
| Retirement | Legacy, travel, gifting | Withdrawal strategies, estate integration |

## Cash Flow \& Wealth Planning

**Integrated Cash Flow Planning**: This allows easy identification of possible budget shortfalls or surpluses, supporting efficient decision-making. Key capabilities include:[^2]

- 360° view of the family's wealth through structured inventory of all assets, liabilities, and income[^3]
- Model future cash flows, run financial scenarios, and assess their impact over time[^3]
- Real-time projection of how investment portfolios will grow over time[^2]
- Budget tracking against lifestyle goals

**Holistic Financial Wellbeing**: Client needs are becoming increasingly individual, demanding holistic advisory services—32% of wealth managers identify this as a key trend. The platform should connect investment decisions directly to life goals, showing that managing money is about dreams, not just digits.[^4][^1]

## Life Event Tracking \& Alerts

**Proactive Life Event Detection**: AI can analyze client behavior and large volumes of data, anticipating life events like retirement, allowing advisors to proactively offer tailored solutions. Key triggers include:[^5]

- Marriage/divorce
- Birth of children
- Job changes/promotions
- Inheritance receipt
- Home purchase/sale
- Business exit
- Health events

**24/7 Monitoring \& Alerts**: As client financial situations or market conditions change, the feasibility of all client goals is automatically updated with off-track signaling and calls to action if needed. Continuous monitoring allows for proactive and more efficient client management at scale.[^2]

## Scenario Analysis \& What-If Planning

**Dynamic Scenario Modeling**: Forward-looking insights based on dynamic economic scenarios support clients in investment decisions, considering an uncertain market environment. Features include:[^2]

- Monte Carlo simulations for goal probability
- What-if analysis for major decisions (e.g., "What if I retire 3 years early?")
- Market downturn stress testing
- Inflation impact modeling
- Tax scenario optimization

**Inheritance Simulations**: Create inheritance simulations and analyze estate implications with precision. This connects lifestyle planning to estate planning for comprehensive wealth transfer analysis.[^3]

## Family \& Relationship Management

**Multi-Generational Planning**: The "Great Wealth Transfer" involves trillions in assets being handed over from Baby Boomers to Gen X and millennials. Key features:[^1]

- Family member profiles and relationships
- Gift tracking history for estate planning[^3]
- Beneficiary designation coordination
- Family dynamics navigation to maintain harmony during wealth planning[^6]

**Collaborative Interface**: A collaborative interface enables continuous enrichment of information by advisors and clients alike, creating a single, trusted data source. This includes secure document sharing with family members and external partners.[^3]

## AI-Powered Personalization

**Generative AI Capabilities**: Gen AI serves as an invaluable assistant in creating goals-based financial plans :


| Feature | Capability |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Risk Management** | Assess risks and provide customized mitigation strategies using advanced analytics |
| **Actionable Recommendations** | Deliver highly personalized advice tailored to specific circumstances |
| **Financial Literacy** | Simplify complex financial concepts, empowering informed decisions |
| **Conversational Interface** | AI-powered chatbots function as virtual financial advisors |
| **Report Generation** | Generate detailed, personalized reports with visual aids |

**Behavioral Finance Integration**: Advisors are using behavioral finance to help clients dodge emotional decisions and stick to their goals. The platform should identify behavioral biases and provide nudges toward rational decision-making.[^1]

## Client Engagement Features

**Mobile-First Experience**: The old-school ways of financial advice—endless emails and hard-to-navigate portals—are outdated. In 2025, it's all about mobile planning apps and digital-first wealth management where clients can check progress while waiting in line for coffee.[^1]

**Progress Tracking Dashboard**: Visual milestone tracking showing:

- Goal completion percentage
- Time to goal achievement
- Required savings rate adjustments
- Portfolio alignment with goals
- Risk-adjusted probability of success

**Personalized Reporting**: Generate branded, tailored reports for each client with continuous updates on key changes and upcoming actions. Track all actions and interactions over time for relationship continuity.[^3]

## Integration Requirements

**Connected Ecosystem**: The lifestyle planning module must integrate with:

- **Investment Management**: Portfolio alignment to goals
- **Retirement Planning**: Long-term projection coordination
- **Estate Planning**: Beneficiary and wealth transfer integration
- **Insurance Planning**: Protection gap analysis for life events
- **Tax Planning**: Tax-efficient goal funding strategies
- **External Accounts**: Account aggregation for complete financial picture[^7]

**API-First Architecture**: The solution should be available through APIs as well as plug-and-play user interfaces, supporting robo-advice client journeys, hybrid advisor experiences, and other technology platforms.[^2]

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
| :-- | :-- |
| Goal achievement rate | >70% |
| Client engagement (monthly active) | >60% |
| Goal completion tracking usage | >80% |
| Client satisfaction (NPS) | >60 |
| Advisor efficiency improvement | >30% |
