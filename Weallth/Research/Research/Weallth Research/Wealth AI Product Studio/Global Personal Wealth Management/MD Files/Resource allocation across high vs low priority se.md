| **Platform instability** | < 99.5% uptime | Shift 20% from features to platform |

## SUMMARY: RESOURCE ALLOCATION PRINCIPLES

### Golden Rules for Segment Resource Allocation[^2][^1][^3]

1. **Follow the Revenue** - 70%+ resources to segments generating 85%+ revenue
2. **Build Once, Customize Many** - Platform investment serves all segments
3. **High Priority Gets Best Talent** - Senior engineers on HNI/UHNWI/FO
4. **Low Priority Gets Leverage** - Reuse, partner, automate for Mass Market
5. **Quarterly Rebalance** - Adjust based on actual performance
6. **Protect Platform** - Never starve shared infrastructure
7. **Customer Success Scales Differently** - High-touch for premium, self-service for mass
