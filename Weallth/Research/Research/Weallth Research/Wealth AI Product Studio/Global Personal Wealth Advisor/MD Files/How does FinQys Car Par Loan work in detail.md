# How does FinQys Car Par Loan work in detail

Based on comprehensive research, here's a **detailed breakdown of how FinQy's Car Par Loan works**:

## Product Overview

**Car Par Loan** is FinQy's flagship vehicle-backed lending product that allows car owners to unlock the financial value of their vehicle while retaining full ownership and use of the car.[^1][^2]


| Aspect | Details |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Product Type** | Loan Against Car (LAC) / Vehicle-backed lending |
| **Maximum LTV** | Up to 200% of car's current market value |
| **Tenure** | Up to 84 months (7 years) |
| **Process** | 100% digital, paperless |
| **Ownership** | Borrower retains 100% ownership and use |


***

## How It Works: Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Instant Car Valuation

```
Customer enters car details on FinQy platform
              ↓
AI-powered valuation engine assesses:
  • Car make and model
  • Year of manufacture
  • Current mileage
  • Condition factors
  • Real-time market data
              ↓
Instant market value displayed
```

**Technology Used:** Real-time market data integration and AI algorithms to determine accurate current market value.[^3]
