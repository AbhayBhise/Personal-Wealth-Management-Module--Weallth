# Car Par Loan vs Traditional Auto Loans: Comprehensive Comparison Document

This document provides a detailed comparison between FinQy's Car Par Loan and traditional auto financing options to help advisors, customers, and stakeholders understand the key differences and positioning.

***

## Executive Summary

Car Par Loan is a **loan against existing car** product that allows vehicle owners to borrow up to **200% of their car's market value** while retaining full ownership and use of the vehicle. This differs fundamentally from traditional auto loans, which are designed to **purchase a new vehicle** with the car serving as collateral until the loan is repaid.[^1][^2]

***

## Product Category Clarification

| Category | Product Type | Primary Purpose |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Car Par Loan** | Loan Against Car (Refinancing) | Unlock liquidity from existing car |
| **Traditional Auto Loan** | Purchase Financing | Buy a new or used car |
| **Personal Loan** | Unsecured Loan | General purpose (can be used for car) |

Car Par Loan competes in the **loan against car/refinancing** segment, not the car purchase financing segment.[^3]

***

## Fundamental Differences

### Loan Purpose \& Structure

| Aspect | Car Par Loan | Traditional Auto Loan |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Purpose** | Get cash from existing car | Purchase a new/used vehicle |
| **Prerequisite** | Must already own a car | No car required |
| **Fund Usage** | Any purpose—education, medical, business, wedding [^2] | Only for vehicle purchase |
| **Collateral** | Existing car (hypothecated) | New car being purchased |
| **Ownership** | Retain full use of car [^4] | Car held as security until loan repaid [^1] |

### Loan-to-Value (LTV) Comparison

| Product | LTV Ratio | Example |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Car Par Loan** | Up to **200%** of car value [^2] | ₹10L car → ₹20L loan |
| **New Car Loan** | 80-100% of car cost [^5] | ₹10L car → ₹8-10L loan |
| **Used Car Loan** | 70-90% of car value [^6] | ₹5L car → ₹3.5-4.5L loan |
| **Personal Loan** | Not asset-linked | Based on income |

The **200% LTV** is Car Par Loan's most significant differentiator—customers can access double their car's value, far exceeding traditional financing limits.[^2]

## Detailed Feature Comparison

### Interest Rates

| Loan Type | Interest Rate Range | Why |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Car Par Loan** | 11.99% - 18% p.a. [^2] | Secured by car, lower risk |
| **New Car Loan** | 8.5% - 12% p.a. [^7] | New asset, lowest risk |
| **Used Car Loan** | 11% - 16% p.a. [^7] | Depreciated asset, higher risk |
| **Personal Loan** | 14% - 24% p.a. [^1] | Unsecured, highest risk |
| **Business Loan** | 19% - 28% p.a. [^2] | Business risk premium |

**Key Insight:** Car Par Loan rates are significantly lower than personal and business loans while offering higher loan amounts.[^2]

### Loan Amount \& Tenure

| Parameter | Car Par Loan | New Car Loan | Used Car Loan | Personal Loan |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Max Amount** | Up to 200% of car value | 100% of car cost | 90% of car value | ₹25-40L (income-based) |
| **Min Amount** | ₹1 Lakh | ₹1 Lakh | ₹1 Lakh | ₹50,000 |
| **Max Tenure** | Up to 84 months (7 years) | Up to 84 months | Up to 60 months | Up to 60 months [^1] |
| **Flexible Tenure** | Yes | Yes | Limited | Yes |

### Eligibility \& Documentation

| Requirement | Car Par Loan | Traditional Auto Loan |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Vehicle Ownership** | Must own car with clear title | Not required |
| **Vehicle Age** | Typically <10 years | N/A (new) or <7 years (used) |
| **Min. Income** | ₹25,000/month | ₹20,000/month |
| **CIBIL Score** | 650+ (flexible) | 700+ typically |
| **Employment** | Salaried/Self-employed/Business | Salaried/Self-employed |
| **Documents** | Aadhaar, PAN, RC only [^2] | 10+ documents typically |

**Car Par Loan Advantage:** Minimal documentation (3 documents) vs. traditional loans requiring extensive paperwork.[^2]

### Processing \& Disbursal

## Risk Comparison

### For Customers

| Risk Factor | Car Par Loan | Traditional Auto Loan | Personal Loan |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Asset at Risk** | Existing car (hypothecated) | New car (hypothecated) | None |
| **Repossession Risk** | Yes, if default | Yes, if default | No asset risk |
| **Impact of Default** | Lose car | Lose car | Credit score damage |
| **Prepayment Penalty** | Typically none/low | May apply | Varies |

### For Lenders

| Risk Factor | Car Par Loan | Traditional Auto Loan |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Collateral Value** | Depreciating asset | Depreciating asset |
| **Recovery Ease** | Car already exists | Car exists |
| **Valuation Risk** | Used car variability | New car = MSRP |
| **Fraud Risk** | Document verification needed | Dealer verification |

## Customer Journey Comparison

### Car Par Loan Journey (FinQy)

```
Day 1: Enter car details → Instant AI valuation → Check eligibility (200% LTV)
Day 1: Upload 3 documents (Aadhaar, PAN, RC) → Instant soft approval
Day 2-3: Verification → Lender matching
Day 4-7: Final approval → Disbursal to bank account
```

**Total Time: 2-7 days**[^2]

### Traditional Auto Loan Journey

```
Day 1: Visit dealer → Select car → Apply for loan
Day 2-3: Submit 10+ documents → Income verification
Day 4-7: Bank verification → Credit check → Property verification
Day 8-10: Approval → Documentation
Day 10-14: Disbursal to dealer
```

**Total Time: 10-14 days**

### Personal Loan Journey

```
Day 1: Apply online → Submit documents
Day 2: Verification → Credit check
Day 3-5: Approval → Disbursal
```

**Total Time: 3-5 days** (but lower amount, higher rate)
