# Client communication templates for presenting goals-based plans

Based on industry best practices and the documentation framework I created earlier, here are **client communication templates for presenting goals-based financial plans**:

## Initial Plan Presentation Templates

### Template 1: Goals Discovery Summary Email

```
Subject: Your Financial Goals - Discovery Session Summary

Dear [Client Name],

Thank you for our productive conversation on [date]. I'm excited to begin 
building your personalized financial plan around the goals that matter 
most to you.

GOALS WE IDENTIFIED:

Priority 1: [Goal Name] - [Target Amount] by [Target Date]
Example: Retirement at 62 - $2.5M portfolio by 2040

Priority 2: [Goal Name] - [Target Amount] by [Target Date]
Example: Children's Education - $250,000 by 2032

Priority 3: [Goal Name] - [Target Amount] by [Target Date]
Example: Vacation Home - $500,000 by 2030

NEXT STEPS:
1. I will analyze your current financial position against these goals
2. Run probability scenarios to determine funding requirements
3. Prepare recommendations for our plan presentation meeting

Our next meeting is scheduled for [date/time] where I'll present your 
complete goals-based financial plan.

Please let me know if any goals or priorities have changed.

Warm regards,
[Advisor Name]
```


### Template 2: Plan Presentation Meeting Agenda

```
[FIRM LOGO]

FINANCIAL PLAN PRESENTATION
Client: [Name]                    Date: [Date]
Prepared by: [Advisor]            Meeting Time: [Time]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

AGENDA (60-90 minutes)

1. WELCOME & OVERVIEW (5 min)
   - Meeting objectives
   - Plan summary preview

2. YOUR GOALS REVIEW (10 min)
   - Confirm prioritized goals
   - Validate target amounts and dates
   - Discuss any life changes

3. CURRENT FINANCIAL SNAPSHOT (10 min)
   - Net worth summary
   - Cash flow analysis
   - Current investment allocation

4. GOALS ANALYSIS & PROJECTIONS (20 min)
   - Goal funding status (funded/underfunded)
   - Monte Carlo simulation results
   - Probability of success for each goal

5. RECOMMENDED STRATEGIES (15 min)
   - Investment allocation changes
   - Savings rate recommendations
   - Tax optimization opportunities

6. SCENARIO COMPARISON (10 min)
   - "What-if" analysis
   - Trade-offs between competing goals
   - Alternative pathways

7. IMPLEMENTATION PLAN (10 min)
   - Action items and timeline
   - Account changes needed
   - Next review date

8. QUESTIONS & DISCUSSION (10 min)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

## Best Practices for Goals-Based Communication

| Element | Recommendation |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Visuals** | Use progress bars, charts, and color-coding (green/yellow/red) for quick comprehension [^1][^6] |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly progress updates, annual comprehensive reviews [^5] |
| **SMART Goals** | Always frame goals as Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound [^5][^6] |
| **Personalization** | Reference specific client goals by name, not generic categories [^7] |
| **Action-Oriented** | Every communication should include clear next steps [^2] |
| **Positive Framing** | Lead with progress made, then address adjustments needed [^9] |
