# Metrics of success in goals-based planning case studies

Based on industry research and platform studies, here are the **key metrics of success in goals-based planning case studies**:

## Client Satisfaction \& Engagement Metrics

Goals-based planning demonstrates significantly higher client satisfaction compared to traditional approaches:


| Metric | Goals-Based | Traditional | Source |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Client satisfaction rate** | 84% | 67% | SEI Investments [^1] |
| **Client satisfaction improvement** | 94% saw improvement | — | eMoney ROI Study [^2] |
| **Client retention improvement** | 81% saw improvement | — | eMoney ROI Study [^2] |

## Business Growth Metrics

### Revenue \& Efficiency Impact

- **92%** of advisors using goals-based planning technology saw improvement in business efficiency[^2]
- **59%** of advisors report advanced technology helps win new clients[^3]
- **51%** gained wallet share with existing clients through better planning tools[^3]
- **90%** of wealth management firms plan to incorporate goals-based wealth management (GBWM) into their offerings[^1]


### Referral Generation

Advisors using interactive goals-based planning tools generated dramatically more referrals:

- **79%** of advisors using interactive planning received over 20 referrals[^2]
- Compared to only **29%** of advisors overall[^2]


## Time \& Productivity Metrics

| Metric | Improvement | Details |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Automated data collection** | 76% reduction | Manual input time eliminated [^3] |
| **Self-service portals** | 40% reduction | Routine service requests decreased [^3] |
| **Client meeting prep** | Minutes vs. days | Digital workflows accelerated [^3] |
| **Time redirected** | 82% | Advisors redirect saved time to deeper client relationships [^3] |

## ROI \& Financial Impact

### Speed to ROI

- **83%** of advisors saw ROI within the first year of implementing goals-based planning software[^2]
- **76%** of advisors using interactive planning realized immediate ROI[^2]
- **73%** saw immediate efficiency improvements in financial plan creation[^2]


### Core Business KPIs to Track

According to eMoney's framework for financial planning firms :

**Growth KPIs:**

- New client acquisition rate
- Revenue from new clients vs. market performance
- Organic growth rate (excluding market gains)

**Retention KPIs:**

- Client retention rate
- Revenue retention rate
- Asset retention across generations

**Client Base KPIs:**

- Average AUM per client
- Average revenue per client
- Revenue concentration analysis

**Advisor Productivity KPIs:**

- Households per advisor
- AUM per advisor
- Revenue per advisor
- Net profit per advisor[^4]


## Goals Achievement Metrics

Unlike traditional benchmarks, goals-based planning measures success against **specific client objectives** rather than market indices:[^5]

- **Goal funding probability** — Monte Carlo simulation success rates (typically targeting 80-90% confidence)
- **Progress toward individual goals** — Tracking retirement, education, home purchase milestones
- **Balance sheet optimization** — Managing assets against current and future liabilities holistically[^6]


## Platform Adoption Success Indicators

| Indicator | Benchmark |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Interactive planning adoption** | Top driver of client satisfaction (69% cite as primary factor) [^2] |
| **Client portal engagement** | Key to quick client satisfaction achievement [^2] |
| **Account aggregation usage** | Essential for seeing held-away assets and family networks [^2] |
| **Plan creation efficiency** | Streamlined data gathering, what-if analysis, presentation [^2] |

## Qualitative Success Indicators

Beyond quantitative metrics, successful implementations demonstrate:

- **Deeper two-way conversations** with clients about life goals[^2]
- **Behavioral coaching effectiveness** during market volatility[^1]
- **Enhanced personalization** fostering stronger client relationships[^1]
- **Competitive differentiation** from product-focused advisors[^1]
- **Advisor positioning as "family CFO"** rather than investment manager[^6]
