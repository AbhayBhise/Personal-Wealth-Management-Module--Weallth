# FinQy growth story from startup to \$9.8M revenue

Based on comprehensive research, here is **FinQy's complete growth story from startup to \$9.8M revenue**:

## Founding Story \& Origin (2019)

### The Inspiration

**Founder:** Manish Aggarwal — Seasoned entrepreneur with **25+ years of BFSI sales experience**[^1]

**The Moment:** The journey began with a personal experience with Manish's trusted insurance agent, Vithalbhai. He noticed a significant gap in how financial intermediaries were serving customers and identified a massive underserved market.[^2]

**Market Opportunity Identified:**

- **3.5 million+ underserved financial intermediaries** in India lacked efficient platforms[^2]
- Complex financial products overwhelming for consumers
- DSAs and agents operating without technology support
- Gap between financial institutions and end customers

**Company Registration:**

- **Legal Entity:** E-REVBAY PRIVATE LIMITED
- **Incorporation Date:** August 8, 2019
- **Location:** Mumbai, Maharashtra, India
- **CIN:** U74999MH2019PTC329097[^3][^4]

## Key Growth Drivers

### 1. Deep Market Understanding

**Founder's Insight:**[^2]
> "What differentiates us from our competition is the ability to understand both the supply and demand side of the business extremely well."

- Deep relationships with financial institutions (supply side)
- In-depth understanding of end-customer concerns (demand side)
- Tech solutions addressing both ends of the business


### 2. Innovative Product Development

| Product | Innovation |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Test My Policy (TMP)** | Automated insurance recommendation engine |
| **Test My Card (TMC)** | Credit card optimization tool |
| **My Loan Bhai (MLB)** | Instant loan eligibility assessment |
| **Car Par Loan** | Up to 200% vehicle value lending |

### 3. Technology-First Approach

- AI-powered sales tools[^1]
- Machine learning for customer insights
- Comprehensive data analytics
- Simple, user-friendly dashboards[^9]


### 4. Partner-Centric Model

| Partner Benefit | Impact |
| :-- | :-- |
| Pre-existing bank codes | No waiting period to start |
| 14-day payout cycle | Improved cash flow |
| Multi-product access | Diversified revenue streams |
| Training \& support | Reduced learning curve |
