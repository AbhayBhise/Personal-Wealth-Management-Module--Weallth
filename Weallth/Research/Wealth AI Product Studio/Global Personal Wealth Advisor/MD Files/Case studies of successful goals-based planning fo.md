# Case studies of successful goals-based planning for advisors

Based on industry research and documented advisor experiences, here are **real-world case studies of successful goals-based planning implementation**:

## Case Study 1: Planful Finance (RightCapital Implementation)

**Background:** Scott Frey, a financial advisor in Oregon, transitioned from MoneyGuidePro to RightCapital in 2018 and later founded Planful Finance, serving clients from their twenties to nineties nationwide.[^1]

**Challenge:** MoneyGuidePro's interface felt like "a relic of the nineties" and required extensive explanation during client meetings, diverting time from actual planning discussions.[^1]

**Solution \& Results:**

- **More meaningful conversations** — RightCapital's visual design let clients "see and digest information so much faster," shifting focus from explaining software to discussing behavioral change[^1]
- **Increased client trust** — A tech industry client who was initially skeptical of MoneyGuidePro showed significantly more confidence using RightCapital[^1]
- **Reduced training overhead** — Minimal instruction needed due to logical layout, allowing focus on mentoring advisors in client interaction rather than software navigation[^1]
- **Higher engagement** — Clients became more engaged when they could visually understand their goals and scenarios simultaneously[^1]

***

## Case Study 2: Silvercrest Family Office (High-Net-Worth Goals Planning)

**Background:** A wealthy client approached Silvercrest with an outdated estate plan—their wills were 10 years old, and their wealth had grown substantially .

**Challenge:** Analysis revealed the client underestimated estate tax liabilities, and existing cash bequests would leave the surviving spouse with inadequate liquidity .

**Goals-Based Approach \& Outcomes:**


| Client Goal | Strategy Implemented | Outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Successful generational wealth transfer | GRAT (Grantor Retained Annuity Trust) | Eliminated appreciation from estate, lowered future taxes, provided specific inheritance amounts for each child |
| Surviving spouse income stream + charitable giving | CRT (Charitable Remainder Trust) | Funded with appreciated asset, provided lifetime income to spouse tax-efficiently, allocated remainder to charity |

**Key Insight:** Silvercrest's holistic approach using customized wealth reports across investment managers, asset classes, and family entities enabled clients to make fully informed decisions .

***

## Case Study 3: Top-5 Independent Broker-Dealer (Advisor Training Program)

**Background:** A major broker-dealer implemented the "Elevate Your Goals-Based Planning" workshop to boost advisor confidence in goals-based planning.[^2]

**Program Components:**

- **Goals Priority Discovery** — In-depth discovery meetings establishing trust and emotional connection before gathering financial data[^2]
- **Alignment training** — Teaching advisors to precisely match recommendations with client goals using planning and investment tools[^2]
- **Onboarding best practices** — Delivering exceptional client experience to confirm advisor selection[^2]

**Outcomes:** Participants reported increased confidence and satisfaction, with specific tools and methods streamlining the planning process to be more efficient and approachable.[^2]

***

## Case Study 4: Fowler Drew (UK-Based Goal Outcomes)

**Background:** UK wealth manager implementing defined-outcome goal-based portfolios.[^3]

**Approach:**

- Assign total resources to multiple goals with prioritization and dependencies
- Create "virtual" goal-based portfolios with specific risk preferences per goal
- Measure portfolio progress forward toward planned outcomes rather than backward performance[^3]

**Key Outcome:** Clients feel in control because constraints and trade-offs are determined by their own probability preferences for achieving outcomes—ensuring solutions are suitable and bias-free.[^3]

***

## Case Study 5: Brown Miller Wealth Management (D.C. Area Fee-Only Firm)

**Background:** Fee-only advisors in Washington D.C. adopting goals-based planning to improve client retention.[^4]

**Results:**

- **Doubled success probability** — Similar to how business plans double business success rates, goals-based financial plans improved client outcomes[^4]
- **Goal discovery** — Implementation process helped clients realize new goals and identify better pathways they hadn't considered[^4]
- **Holistic integration** — Covering wealth management, tax, and insurance planning provided clients with top-down understanding of their financial picture[^4]

**Key Insight:** Goals-based planning is "more customized, more personal, more engaged" because clients define multiple prioritized goals organized by time horizon rather than pursuing a single end-all objective.[^4]

***

## Common Success Factors Across Cases

| Factor | Evidence |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Visual clarity** | RightCapital's interface eliminated need to explain software, freeing time for planning [^1] |
| **Emotional connection** | Discovery meetings building trust before data gathering improved outcomes [^2] |
| **Reduced emotional decisions** | Tying investments to specific goals prevented panic-selling during volatility [^5] |
| **Client control** | Letting clients set probability preferences for outcomes increased satisfaction [^3] |
| **Holistic view** | Integrated planning across tax, estate, and investments enabled informed decisions |
