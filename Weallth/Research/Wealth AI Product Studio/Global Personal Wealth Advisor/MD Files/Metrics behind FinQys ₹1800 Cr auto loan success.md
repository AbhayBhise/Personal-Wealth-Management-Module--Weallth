## Key Success Drivers

### 1. Innovative Product: Car Par Loan

FinQy's flagship auto loan product offers unique features that differentiate it from competitors:[^2][^3]


| Feature | Details |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Loan-to-Value Ratio** | Up to **200% of car's current market value** |
| **Process** | 100% digital, no paperwork, no branch visits |
| **Approval** | Instant soft approval online |
| **Tenure** | Up to **84 months** |
| **Eligibility** | Salaried, self-employed, and business owners |
| **Asset Retention** | 100% ownership stays with borrower |

**Industry Context:** Traditional loan-against-car products typically offer only 80-100% of vehicle value. FinQy's 200% offering (subject to lender criteria) represents a significant innovation.[^4]

### 2. Digital-First Approach

| Capability | Impact |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Instant Car Valuation** | Real-time market value assessment |
| **Instant Loan Eligibility** | Immediate calculation of eligible amount |
| **Online Documentation** | Upload documents digitally |
| **No Branch Visits** | Completely paperless process |
| **Instant Soft Approval** | No manual follow-ups required |

**2025 Innovation:** FinQy reimagined vehicle-backed lending by merging AI, seamless digital processes, and full platform transparency—accelerating loan approvals and disbursements.[^5]
