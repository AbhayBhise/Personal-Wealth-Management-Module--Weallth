## Detailed Platform Reviews

### RightCapital — Best Overall for Goals-Based Planning

**Industry Position:** Market leader with highest advisor satisfaction ratings

**Kitces Market-Leading Categories:** Overall Satisfaction, Tax Planning, Client Portal, Customer Support, Account Aggregation, Customization, Plan Delivery, Ease/Simplicity

**Key Strengths:**

- Flexible planning approach (cash-flow, goals-based, or modified cash-flow)[^1]
- Superior tax planning capabilities with tax-sensitive decumulation strategies
- Modern, intuitive interface built with current technology
- Strong visuals rating (8.5 vs. MoneyGuide's 7.2)[^1]
- Includes budgeting, debt management, and student loan tools[^1]

**Pricing:** Starts at \$124.95/month or \$495/year[^2]

**Adoption Rate:** 78% of XYPN members use RightCapital as their primary planning tool[^3]

***

### eMoney Advisor — Best for High-Net-Worth Clients

**Industry Position:** Comprehensive solution for complex planning needs

**Kitces Market-Leading Categories:** Client Portal, Customization, Depth/Comprehensiveness, Retirement Accumulation, Retirement Decumulation

**Key Strengths:**

- Comprehensive financial planning modules covering retirement, tax, estate, and insurance[^4]
- Client-facing digital tools with secure portals and interactive planning[^4]
- Predictive modeling and forecasting powered by analytics[^4]
- Seamless integration with custodians, CRM systems, and portfolio management tools[^4]
- Strong account aggregation capabilities

**Best For:** Advisors serving high-net-worth clients with complex, multi-generational planning needs[^5]

**Adoption Rate:** 16% of XYPN members[^3]

***

### MoneyGuide — Pioneer in Goals-Based Planning

**Industry Position:** The original goals-based planning platform, now part of Envestnet ecosystem[^6]

**Planning Approach:** Pure goals-based methodology (not cash-flow based)[^6]

**Key Strengths:**

- Focus on helping clients prioritize and fund their most important goals
- Flexible platform supporting foundational budgeting to sophisticated UHNW strategies[^6]
- Play Zone® feature for testing variables interactively
- Holistic stress testing capabilities
- Strong integration within Envestnet ecosystem[^6]

**Key Features:**

- Social Security optimization
- Advanced lifetime protection analysis
- Custom capital market assumptions
- Retirement distribution planning
- Risk tolerance assessment

**Adoption Rate:** 4% of XYPN members prefer pure goals-based approach[^3]

***

### NaviPlan — Best for Complex Scenario Analysis

**Industry Position:** Known for robust scenario analysis and detailed planning[^5]

**Kitces Market-Leading Categories:** Tax Planning, Customization, Depth/Comprehensiveness, Retirement Accumulation, Retirement Decumulation

**Key Strengths:**

- Exceptional side-by-side scenario comparison capabilities[^5]
- Intuitive dialogue boxes making data entry easy for clients and advisors[^5]
- Comprehensive planning for retirement, education, and estate goals[^5]
- Monte Carlo analysis with Morningstar Capital Markets assumptions[^5]
- Easy modeling of major financial changes (selling house, estate planning changes)[^5]

**Key Features:**

- Advanced tax planning
- Equity compensation planning
- Business planning modules
- Collaborative financial planning
- Compliance workflow tools

**Best For:** Advisors handling complex financial situations requiring detailed customization[^5]

***

### AdvisorEngine — Best for Unified Planning \& Implementation

**Industry Position:** Integrated platform connecting planning to execution[^7]

**Unique Value Proposition:** Create, fund, and implement financial plans within a unified platform through partnership with Franklin Templeton[^7]

**Key Strengths:**

- Seamless planning-to-portfolio implementation (no system jumping)[^7]
- Automated trading and rebalancing aligned with goals[^7]
- Two planning approaches: Core (traditional) and Dynamic (multi-period optimization)[^7]
- Held-away account incorporation for complete financial picture[^7]
- Client self-service portal for building goals-based plans[^7]

**Alerts \& Monitoring:**

- Missed contribution alerts
- Unexpected withdrawal notifications
- Plans falling below probability thresholds[^7]

**Best For:** Advisors wanting unified planning and portfolio management without switching systems[^7]

***

### Asset-Map — Best for Visual Financial Mapping

**Industry Position:** Visual-first approach to financial planning

**Kitces Market-Leading Categories:** Plan Delivery, Insurance, Ease/Simplicity

**Key Strengths:**

- Creates comprehensive visual maps of client financial situations
- Identifies gaps and opportunities quickly
- Easy communication of complex concepts
- Strong collaboration features
- Works alongside cash-flow or goals-based planning tools

**Best For:** Advisors who want visual discovery and presentation tools to complement primary planning software

***

### MoneyTree — Best for Retirement Specialists

**Industry Position:** Established solution with focus on retirement planning

**Kitces Market-Leading Categories:** Retirement Accumulation, Retirement Decumulation

**Key Features:**

- Interactive what-if scenario tools
- One-page summary reports
- Specialized tools for education, estate, and life insurance planning
- Audit trail for compliance

**Pricing:** Starts at \$229-\$495/year[^2]

## Key Selection Criteria

When choosing goals-based planning software, prioritize these seven features :

1. **Intuitive user experience** — Minimizes training time, maximizes productivity
2. **Comprehensive planning capabilities** — Retirement, tax, estate, education, insurance
3. **Strong integration ecosystem** — CRM, custodian, performance reporting connectivity
4. **Engaging client experience** — Interactive portal with collaborative planning tools
5. **Scenario modeling flexibility** — Easy creation and comparison of multiple scenarios
6. **Ongoing innovation** — Regular updates addressing regulatory changes
7. **Solid customer support** — Responsive help via chat, email, phone, and plan reviews
