# RESOURCE REALLOCATION STRATEGIES POST-MVP LAUNCH

## Global Wealth Management Platform - Dynamic Resource Optimization Guide

**Date:** January 24, 2026
**Purpose:** Define frameworks and triggers for reallocating resources after each segment MVP launch
**Scope:** Engineering, Sales, CS, Product, and Design teams across all wealth segments

***

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Post-MVP Resource Reallocation Philosophy

The transition from MVP to growth phase requires fundamentally different resource allocation:


| Phase | Primary Focus | Resource Split | Key Metric |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Pre-MVP** | Build core features | 90% Build / 10% Support | Feature completion |
| **MVP Launch (Week 1-4)** | Stabilize \& fix | 60% Fix / 30% Support / 10% Build | Bug count, uptime |
| **Post-MVP (Month 1-3)** | Iterate \& retain | 50% Iterate / 30% Support / 20% Next | NPS, retention |
| **Growth (Month 3+)** | Scale \& expand | 40% Current / 30% Next / 30% Scale | Revenue, growth |

### Reallocation Trigger Summary

| Trigger Type | Example | Action |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Success Trigger** | Exceeded adoption targets | Accelerate next segment, reduce current |
| **Struggle Trigger** | Below adoption targets | Double down or pivot resources |
| **Stability Trigger** | Product stable, low bugs | Shift to next segment |
| **Market Trigger** | Competitive pressure | Reallocate to threatened segment |
| **Revenue Trigger** | Segment outperforming | Increase investment in winner |

### 6.2 Reallocation Velocity Guidelines

| Transition Type | Timeline | Ramp Down | Ramp Up | Risk |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Same Domain (HNI→UHNWI)** | 2-4 weeks | Fast | Fast | Low |
| **Adjacent Domain (UHNWI→FO)** | 4-6 weeks | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| **Different Domain (FO→MM)** | 6-8 weeks | Slow | Slow | High |
| **Emergency Pivot** | 1-2 weeks | Immediate | Immediate | Very High |

### 6.3 Knowledge Transfer Protocol

Before any resource transition:


| Step | Timeline | Owner | Deliverables |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **1. Documentation** | Week 1 | Outgoing team | Architecture docs, runbooks |
| **2. Shadowing** | Week 1-2 | Both teams | Pairing sessions |
| **3. Handoff** | Week 2 | Outgoing lead | Formal handoff meeting |
| **4. Support Period** | Week 2-4 | Outgoing team | Slack channel, office hours |
| **5. Clean Break** | Week 4+ | New team | Full ownership |

### 6.4 Reallocation Checklist

#### Pre-Reallocation (1 week before)

- [ ] Success metrics reviewed and documented
- [ ] Receiving team requirements defined
- [ ] Knowledge transfer plan created
- [ ] Backlog groomed for transition
- [ ] On-call rotation updated
- [ ] Access permissions mapped


#### During Reallocation (Transition week)

- [ ] Architecture walkthrough completed
- [ ] Code review sessions done
- [ ] Key contacts introduced
- [ ] Documentation verified
- [ ] Test environments handed over
- [ ] Monitoring dashboards shared


#### Post-Reallocation (2 weeks after)

- [ ] New team operating independently
- [ ] No critical escalations to old team
- [ ] Velocity returning to normal
- [ ] Stakeholder communication complete
- [ ] Retrospective conducted

| Stakeholder | When to Inform | How | Who Communicates |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |

| **Customers (if impacted)** | If service changes | Email/call | CS Lead |
| **Partners** | If relationship changes | Partner call | Partner Manager |

### 9.2 Team Member Transition Communication

**Script for Manager-Team Member Conversation:**

> "As we've successfully launched [Segment] MVP and achieved our targets, we're now shifting focus to [Next Segment]. Based on your skills in [specific skill], we'd like you to transition to the [Next Segment] team where you'll be working on [specific project]. This is a recognition of your strong performance and an opportunity to grow your experience. The transition will happen over [timeline]. Do you have any questions or concerns?"

### 9.3 Resistance Handling

| Resistance Type | Response Strategy |
| :-- | :-- |
| **"I want to stay on current segment"** | Discuss growth opportunity, offer hybrid role if possible |
| **"I don't have skills for new segment"** | Provide training plan, pair with experienced team member |
| **"Timeline is too fast"** | Extend transition period if possible, provide more support |
| **"New segment isn't interesting"** | Highlight business importance, connect to career goals |
| **"Will this affect my performance review?"** | Clarify expectations, adjust goals for transition period |

### 12.2 Reallocation Principles Summary

1. **Never leave a segment unstaffed** - Minimum maintenance team always remains
2. **Transition in cohorts** - Move teams together, not individuals
3. **Allow ramp time** - 2-4 weeks for productivity recovery
4. **Communicate early** - Inform team members 2+ weeks before transition
5. **Document everything** - Knowledge transfer is mandatory
6. **Monitor velocity** - Track and address productivity dips
7. **Maintain morale** - Frame as growth opportunity, not displacement
8. **Be flexible** - Adjust based on actual performance, not just plan
